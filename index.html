STITCHING & ALTERATIONS

A better fit is
in the details.

A good fit, thoughtful finishing and the little details that make a piece feel yours. Explore the work we can help with.

01

Custom stitching

Blouses, suits, lehengas and occasionwear, tailored to your measurements and style.

What to bring +

A reference picture, a garment that already fits you well, or your fabric if you have it. Measurements and any extra requirements can be discussed at the shop.

Enquire about stitching ↗
02

Alterations & restyling

Fit adjustments, fall and pico, resizing, and giving favourite pieces a second life.

What to bring +

Bring the actual garment and explain what needs adjusting. The boutique can discuss what is feasible after seeing the fabric and construction.

Enquire about alterations ↗
03

Stitching classes

Practical sewing skills, from first seams to garment construction. Ask about available batches.

What to ask +

Ask for the next batch dates, fees, class levels and whether you need to bring any materials. Places and schedules must be confirmed with the boutique.
